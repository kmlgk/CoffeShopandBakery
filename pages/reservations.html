Before You Book

Reservation Questions

Do you charge for cancellations?
No — this is a request, not a paid booking. Just let us know if your plans change so we can free up the table.
Can I just walk in instead?
Always welcome — walk-ins are seated first-come, first-served. Reserving just guarantees a table at busy times.
What if my party is larger than 8?
Give us a call — we can often combine tables or set up the long table for bigger groups and events.
How long do you hold the table?
15 minutes past your reserved time. After that we may release it, so a quick call if you're running late helps.
